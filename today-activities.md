## Extra ideas to try if there is time

- Add different audio layers for indoor and outdoor areas.
- Add a subtle sound or visual effect when the player discovers a clue or secret item.
- Improve footsteps so they change based on the floor or room.
- Add screen shake or a small flash for powerful weapon impacts, while keeping it comfortable for the player.
- Add a short equip animation and sound when switching weapons.
- Add an audio cue when an interaction is available nearby.
- Add NPC dialogue, reactions, or missions that help guide the player through each level.
- Plan level themes, objectives, puzzles, and progression for additional rooms or floors.
- Check that all audio has sensible volume levels and does not distract from important clues.
- Create a reusable animation or audio helper so future weapons can be added faster.
- Test keyboard controls, restart behavior, and room transitions after the new changes.
- Capture a short before-and-after comparison for the animations and weapon audio.
